Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. The ProCierge app's own Privacy Policy will cover how the app handles information about children, since that is where family accounts actually exist — and children in ProCierge are only ever visible to their own verified parents.

How We Use This Information

Only to respond to inquiries, to notify waiting-list members about ProCierge, and to maintain and improve this website. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Removing Your Information

Want off the waiting list, or want your information deleted? Email us and we'll take care of it.
